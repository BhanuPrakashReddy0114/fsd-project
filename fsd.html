A FEW HELPFUL THINGS

Lost & found, thoughtfully.

01
Report what you can

Include a clear description, date, and campus zone. A photo helps, but isn't required.

02
Keep a detail private

Don't share serial numbers, passcodes, engravings, or personal documents in a public post.

03
Verify in a safe place

Arrange handover at the Northwood Campus Security Desk, Main Building ground floor. Bring your campus ID.

04
Found something valuable?

Turn in electronics, wallets, and IDs directly to campus security. This demo does not deliver messages or store claims on a server.
